# Source trust labels: the host list for your review (2026-10-23)

For FEAT-20261023-1130 (`docs/specs/FEAT-20261023-1130-source-trust-labels.md`). Nothing is built until you approve this list. It is based on the 1,189 pages and 755 supported claims in our database (132 hosts support claims, 236 hosts were cited).

## The three tiers

- **official**: the organisation that runs the competition or the team: a league, a tour, a federation, a tournament, a club. It is the primary source for its own fixtures, line-ups and injuries.
- **news**: an independent news or sports-media organisation with an editorial desk.
- **other**: everything else, including every host not on this list: betting and odds sites, tipster and prediction sites, prediction markets, statistics databases, forums, open encyclopedias. **Unknown hosts are always "other"; nothing is trusted by default.**

A subdomain counts as its host (`de.uefa.com` is `uefa.com`). "official" is a statement about who runs the site, not a guarantee that a page is true or current.

## How the list stays current

New club and tournament sites appear with every league we add, so the list will always be incomplete. The build will include a read-only command, `source-hosts`, that prints the hosts in saved runs that are not on the list yet, with their counts, so you can add them. Until a host is added it is "other", which is the safe direction.

## What I could not verify

I classified from the host names and the page titles saved in our database. I did not open the sites. The news list holds outlets I know to be news organisations, but most did not appear in our runs. A club or tournament host is on the list because its page titles say so.
