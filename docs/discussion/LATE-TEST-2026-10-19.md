# Late-game price test: reading (2026-10-19)

Spec: `docs/specs/FEAT-20261019-1231-late-game-price-test.md`. Raw table: `LATE-TEST-RAW-2026-10-19.md`. Free, read-only, nothing ordered.

## Result by the rule written before the run

**Not found: "too few bets".** Of 2,250 games (baseball, basketball, tennis; the eight soccer leagues were left out because their close time is a fixed 120 minutes after the start), only 153 bets qualified at the 5-minute entry: 48 in the first half and 105 in the second. The rule asks for at least 100 in each half, so no verdict can be given. This is a result in itself: even with hindsight, a 10 to 40 cent contract with a tight spread and real trading five minutes before the end is rare (about 1 bet in 15 games).

## The numbers that look good, and why they are not evidence

Shown as descriptive only: the average result of buying NO is +12 to +15 cents per contract at 5 and 10 minutes (second half at 5 minutes: +14.6 cents, 99% interval +9.6 to +18.2), mostly tennis (83 of 105 bets). I do **not** read this as an edge:

1. **The "last 5 minutes" is chosen with hindsight.** The close time is the real end of the match. A game that ends in 5 minutes is, by that fact, nearly decided, and the side priced 10 to 40 cents is almost always the side that loses. A trader cannot know which games are ending. The same selection would produce this result in a perfectly fair market.
2. The spec said in advance that a pass could only earn a second spec for a tradable trigger, and the rule did not pass anyway.
3. No depth data, minute closes are not fills, and none of the bets had an unchanged quote (0 of 153), so stale quotes are not the explanation.

## Decision

- No claim to the client of an in-game opportunity. What can be said: a published study suggests late-game mispricing; we tested it on Kalshi history, the rule needed more bets than exist, and the numbers that exist can be explained by the hindsight selection.
- A tradable version would need a trigger that does not use the close time (the clock since the start plus a live score) and fresh data not used to choose the rule. We have no live score source. **Not started; a second spec would be needed first.** The small number of qualifying bets (about 1 per 15 games, mostly tennis) also limits what it could ever earn.
- Not done and not to be done on this data: trying other minutes, price bands or sports after seeing these numbers (the spec forbids it).
